Artificial
Intelligence

From first principles: the mathematics, the learning, and the machines that think.

Soliman M.H.

A complete treatment, built from the ground up. Every prerequisite is taught here. Nothing is assumed that is not first established, and nothing is asserted that could be derived.

All explanatory material, figures, worked examples, drills and projects are original. Every figure is generated by code that performs the computation it depicts.

Thirty-four parts. The book was built to grow without rewriting what came before, and nothing already written has had to change as it did.
