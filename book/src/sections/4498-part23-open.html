why it works, and where the guarantees run out

Part VI taught capacity and Part V taught estimation, and the machinery joining them, the theorems that say when learning from a finite sample works at all, was never built. Here it is, together with the measurement showing that the classical guarantees are numerically worthless at the sizes anyone actually uses, and what replaced them. Then the other limit: not more data, but more width, where the network becomes solvable and stops being interesting.
