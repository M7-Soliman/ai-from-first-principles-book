Part X taught convolution and stopped at classification. Almost nothing deployed stops there: a driving stack wants where things are, how far away, and which one is which from frame to frame. Each of those is a different output space with a different metric and a different way of being quietly wrong, and two of them are limited by geometry rather than by the model.
