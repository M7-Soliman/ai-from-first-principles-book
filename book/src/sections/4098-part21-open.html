what we can see, what we can specify, and what we can prove

Part XX built the systems and Part XVIII supplied the training machinery. This part asks the three questions that remain: can we see what a model computes, can we say what we want from it, and can we establish that a deployed system is safe. The honest answers are partial, and saying exactly how partial is most of the work.
