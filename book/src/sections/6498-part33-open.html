the other tradition, and why it came back

A model that maps an input to an output in one pass can only be as good as one pass. Everything in this part is about spending more computation at the moment of use: enumerating possibilities, pruning most of them, and choosing among what survives. It was the whole of the field before learning worked, then it was unfashionable, and it is now half of every reasoning system that works.
